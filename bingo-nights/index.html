A SIMPLE SETUP

What everyone pays

Two different payments, kept completely separate.

The pub pays Chip In

£150 standard hosting fee for organising and running the five-game evening. Optional enhancements are agreed separately. This is not taken from ticket sales or prize money.

Players pay for their bingo

£5 for one card in each of the five games (£1 stake per game). All player stakes are paid back in prizes. No separate bingo entry or participation charge.

VENUE CHECKLIST

What the pub needs to provide

  • An appropriate licensed venueThe venue confirms it is eligible to run exempt bingo and has a designated premises supervisor responsible for compliance.
  • Suitable space and powerTables, seats and a usable area for calling the games.
  • The usual venue serviceYour staff look after food, drinks and normal operations.
  • Any required music permissionsWe confirm the permissions relevant to music used at the venue before the event.

Important: This package is for adults aged 18+ at eligible venues, operating as exempt bingo. No admission or participation fee is charged for the bingo. The maximum stake is £5 per person per game; the £2,000 seven-day stakes/prizes threshold and other applicable rules must be respected. The venue remains responsible for its premises compliance, with the details confirmed before booking. Read the Gambling Commission's pub bingo guidance.
