BINGO, BY THE BOOK

All the fun. None of the legal headaches.

Traditional bingo can usually be hosted at eligible alcohol-licensed pubs without a separate bingo operating licence, as long as the rules are followed. Here is the easy version for our five-game night.

Up to £5 per player, per game

With five games in an evening, the full £5 on each game would add up to £25 per person. That is not a separate legal nightly cap — the statutory limit is per game. Our usual format is just £1 per game, or £5 for all five.

The stakes become the prizes

No extra bingo admission or participation fee, and no deductions from stakes or winnings. Every penny staked goes back to prizes. The pub pays my hosting fee separately, never from the prize pot.

Keep an eye on the seven-day limit

For exempt pub bingo, stakes or prizes must stay within the £2,000 threshold in any seven days. Higher-turnover bingo has extra notification and licensing requirements, so we check the numbers in advance.

Adults only, in a suitable pub

Players must be 18 or older. Games cannot be linked to other venues or played online. The pub must have an appropriate alcohol premises licence, and its designated premises supervisor remains responsible for compliance.

The idea is simple: fair games, a properly organised prize pot and a great evening. For the full legal explanation, visit the Gambling Commission's official guide to bingo in pubs.
