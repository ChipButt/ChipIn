08 / HOW THE PROJECT RUNS

Agree payments, timing and approval.

Don't assume payment collection is automatic. Record what's agreed, and follow up with a proper Chip In HQ quote and invoice.

Chip In's next steps

Confirm the final written scope and price, send the quote, arrange payment, gather assets, build, provide draft for review and prepare launch.

Client's next steps

Approve the quote, arrange payment as agreed, provide the outstanding content and access, review the first draft and sign off for launch.

Explain the payment process in four simple steps

  1. After this meeting: Chip In sends the written quotation showing the £250 build, any extras, and their selected support plan.
  2. Before work or domain purchase: The client confirms acceptance and receives an invoice with the agreed due date and payment instructions.
  3. At launch: Chip In confirms that the site is approved, hosting is arranged, and the client knows how to make basic edits.
  4. After launch: If they choose a care plan, recurring billing starts on the agreed date. The £20 managed domain is billed annually. Neither payment is automatically collected by this meeting tool.
Website design does not include domain, hosting or paid external tools unless separately agreed. You cannot mark this quote as fully fixed while a necessary hosting cost or custom feature remains unpriced.
