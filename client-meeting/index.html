08 / HOW THE PROJECT RUNS

Agree payments, timing and approval.

Don't assume payment collection is automatic. Record what's agreed, and follow up with a proper Chip In HQ quote and invoice.

PROJECT MILESTONES · AGREED WITH CLIENT

Agree the website delivery timetable

The client commits to the latest assets deadline in Step 5. Chip In's build period is agreed in calendar days, starting only once all required photos, assets and content have actually arrived. Review and revision windows then follow automatically.

Client review: 3 calendar days from the first draft to send one consolidated amendment list. Chip In revisions: 5 working days (Monday–Friday; UK bank holidays may extend this) after the client's feedback deadline. The revised, finished version is then presented for final written approval. Publication: normally 24–48 hours after that approval, subject to domain DNS and SSL propagation. This is not a guarantee of the time third-party systems take.

Chip In's next steps

Agree the assets deadline and build duration, confirm the scope and payment, start the build only after complete assets are received, issue a first draft, complete one consolidated revision round within five working days, and request written launch approval.

Client's next steps

Approve the quote and payment, supply all agreed photos/assets by the latest date, return one consolidated set of feedback within three days of the draft, then provide written approval of the finished version for launch.

Explain the payment process in four simple steps

  1. After this meeting: Chip In sends the written quotation showing the £250 build, any extras, and their selected support plan.

  3. At launch: Chip In shows the revised finished website and obtains final written approval. The domain goes live normally within 24–48 hours after approval, subject to third-party DNS and SSL changes.
  4. After launch: If they choose a care plan, recurring billing starts on the agreed date. The optional £20 managed domain is billed annually; standard static hosting is £0. Neither payment is automatically collected by this meeting tool.
