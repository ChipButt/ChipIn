HOW IT WORKS

A quiz night built around your venue and your crowd.

Whether you need the whole thing written and hosted or already have the questions ready to go, the aim is the same: a smooth night that is easy for the venue to run and enjoyable for the teams taking part.

01

Plan the night

We talk through the venue, date, likely crowd, number of rounds and the sort of tone or difficulty you want.

02

The quiz gets prepared

I write and build the rounds where needed, including themed, picture or music content, and set up QuizPro digital answering as standard.

03

I run the room

On the night I handle the questions, pacing, teams and scoring so the venue can focus on everything else.

04

Winners, scores, done

The scores are kept organised through the night so the finish, results and winner announcement stay simple and clear.
