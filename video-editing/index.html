From raw footage to a clear, polished finished piece, or simple filming and editing from the ground up. I can help with business videos, promos, welcome videos, events, personal projects and content that just needs shaping properly.

HOW IT WORKS

From rough idea or raw footage to a finished video.

You can come to me with a clear brief, a folder full of clips, or simply an idea of what the finished piece needs to achieve. I can take care of the parts you need and keep the process straightforward.

01

Tell me what you’re making

We pin down the purpose, audience, finished length and where the video will be used, along with any footage or assets you already have.

02

Footage comes together

You can supply the footage, or I can handle simple local filming where the project needs it.

03

I build the edit

I shape the pacing, structure, titles, graphics and overall finish into something clear, watchable and suited to the job.

04

Review & delivery

You see the finished direction, we deal with any agreed final changes, and I deliver the completed video in the format you need.
