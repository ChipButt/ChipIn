WHY CHOOSE CHIP?

A lot of different hats. Years of actually wearing them.

Chip In might be a new name, but the experience behind it has been built over a long time. I’ve spent more than 15 years in hospitality, working from the front line through to management, so I know what busy shifts, short staffing, customers, suppliers, stock, rotas and the hundred little jobs that keep a business running actually look like.

Alongside that, I’ve spent years working creatively and technically — editing video and audio, creating digital content, working with websites and business systems, photography, design and solving the sort of practical tech problems that are much easier when someone can just get on with them.

I’m also a co-founder of Roll Britannia, an award-winning podcast, where I’ve worked as an editor, producer and voice actor, as well as helping deliver live shows, events, merchandise and promotional content. Add a background in performing arts, presenting and live performance, and I’ve ended up equally comfortable behind the bar, behind a microphone, behind a camera or behind a computer.

That slightly unusual mix is exactly the point of Chip In: you don’t always need another employee or a specialist agency — sometimes you just need someone adaptable, experienced and happy to get stuck in. That’s where I Chip In.
